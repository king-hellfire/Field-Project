Keyboard shortcuts

Toggle dark mode AltShiftD
Toggle focus (reading) layout AltShiftF
Reset all styles on the page AltShiftR

Change these anytime at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device. Themes, settings and your API key are stored locally. Page text is sent to Google's Gemini API only when you run an AI tool, and nothing is ever sold or tracked.
